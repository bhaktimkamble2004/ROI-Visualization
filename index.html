Interactive Geospatial Visualization of ROI Using Landsat 9, Sentinel-2 and MODIS NDVI

An interactive geospatial web application for comparing surface reflectance and vegetation index products across three sensors — one dataset in view at a time, fully synchronised metadata, legend and interpretation.

Study Context

This study focuses on the coastal talukas of Dapoli, Khed, and Chiplun in Ratnagiri district, Maharashtra. These regions represent a diverse landscape of coastal ecosystems, agriculture, settlements, and forest cover, making them ideal for multi-source satellite data analysis.

Objective

To visualize and compare multi-source satellite datasets (Landsat 9, Sentinel-2, and MODIS NDVI) for the study area and analyze the spatial patterns of land cover, vegetation health, and surface dynamics over a multi-temporal period.

About the Study Area
  • Topography

    Coastal plains, hills and undulating terrain.

  • Land Cover

    Forests, agriculture, settlements, water bodies and barren land.

  • Climate

    Tropical monsoon climate with heavy seasonal rainfall.

  • Relevance

    Supports environmental monitoring and land-use planning.

Note: The area extent is approximate and based on the union of Dapoli, Khed and Chiplun talukas.
